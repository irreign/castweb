Termite control & pre-purchase inspection

Termite jobs range from a S$150 spot treatment to a S$3,500 whole-property baiting programme — the widest price range of any pest job in Singapore, because the right answer depends entirely on inspection findings. If you're buying a resale flat or landed property, this is also the one pest issue worth checking before you commit, not after.

The three treatment tiers

  • Spot treatment — localised infestation, one or two access points, fastest and cheapest.
  • Soil treatment — a chemical barrier around the property's perimeter/foundation, used for more established ground-level activity.
  • Baiting systems — in-ground or above-ground bait stations that target the whole colony over weeks to months, used for severe or hard-to-reach infestations.

Termite jobs are almost always priced only after a physical inspection — a quote given without one is a red flag, not a shortcut.

Buying a resale flat or landed property?

A pre-purchase termite inspection is a considered, one-off decision tied to your transaction timeline — not a panic. It's the cheapest insurance in a property purchase this size: a S$150–500 inspection against a structural repair bill that can run into the tens of thousands if termite damage is found after you've moved in.

FAQ

Common questions

How do I know if I have termites?
Mud tubes along walls or foundations, hollow-sounding timber, discarded wings near light fixtures, and visible damage to skirting or door frames are the common signs. If you're unsure, a professional inspection is worth it before committing to any treatment.
Can I get an inspection before I sign the purchase agreement?
Yes — this is exactly when a pre-purchase inspection adds the most value, while you can still negotiate on price or require treatment as a condition of sale. Tell us your timeline in the form and we'll flag it as time-sensitive.
Is one treatment enough, or do I need an ongoing contract?
Depends on severity and treatment type. Baiting systems in particular often involve ongoing monitoring visits over several months. Your matched operator will set this out after inspection.
