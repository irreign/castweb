SFA assesses each premises individually at inspection. Use this to understand the baseline before you talk to a vendor — always confirm final scope against your actual SFA licence conditions.

What SFA actually requires

  • A valid pest control contract is a condition of an SFA food shop licence application — without one, the application isn't approved.
  • Minimum pest coverage: cockroaches, rodents and flies.
  • Minimum inspection frequency: monthly, sustained across a 12-month contract term.
  • The operator carrying out the work must be NEA-certified.

Based on published SFA food-shop-licence and NEA pest-control-industry requirements as summarised by licensed pest control operators; confirm current requirements directly with SFA/NEA or your appointed operator.
