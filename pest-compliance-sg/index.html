Why this exists

Pest control in Singapore isn't optional for most of our visitors — it's regulation

If you're a homeowner with a one-off cockroach problem, plenty of directories can point you to a vendor. PestPass SG exists for the buyers regulation puts on a deadline.

§

Your SFA licence needs it

A valid pest control contract — covering cockroaches, rodents and flies at minimum, inspected monthly — is a requirement of an SFA food shop licence application. Without one, your application isn't approved.

⌂

Your MCST already budgets for it

Common-area pest control is a standard line item most MAs procure every contract cycle. The question is whether you're getting a vendor that fits your estate size, not whether you need one.

!

NEA notices come with a deadline

A mosquito-breeding warning letter under the Destruction of Disease Bearing Insects Act is time-boxed. We route these requests to partners who can respond fast.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is PestPass SG a pest control company?
No. We're a referral network — we match your request with NEA-certified operators from our partner list. Treatments, quotes, and contracts are between you and the operator you choose.
Does this cost anything?
Requesting a match is free. Operators pay us for qualified introductions, not the other way around.
How fast will I hear back?
For urgent requests (an active infestation, an NEA deadline, a licence application in progress) we aim to route your request the same day. Routine enquiries are typically matched within 1–2 business days.
What if I just want a one-off treatment, not a contract?
That's fine — tell us in the form. Not every job needs a recurring contract; our partners handle one-off residential treatments too.
