The rule, in plain terms

What SFA is actually checking for

§

Coverage

At minimum, cockroaches, rodents and flies must be included in your pest control contract. Food factories and larger sites are often assessed for stored-product insects too.

📅

Frequency

Inspections must take place at least monthly, sustained for the full contract term — a one-off treatment doesn't satisfy the requirement.

✓

Operator

The company carrying out the work needs to be NEA-certified. NEA regulates the pest control industry; SFA sets and enforces the hygiene standard.

Summarised from SFA food-shop-licence guidance and industry references. Confirm current requirements directly with SFA or your appointed operator before renewal.

Why it matters

The cost of getting this wrong isn't the contract — it's the licence

Pest-related findings during an SFA inspection can lead to corrective-action requirements, suspension, or in repeat/severe cases, licence revocation. A S$100–300/month contract is cheap insurance against losing the ability to trade.

  • New licence applications need a contract in place before approval
  • Renewals and inspections check the contract is still active and being honoured
  • A failed inspection often means switching vendors under time pressure — exactly when a rushed, uninformed choice is most likely
