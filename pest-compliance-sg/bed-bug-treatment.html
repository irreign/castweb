Bed bug treatment

For a homeowner, bed bugs are unpleasant. For a hotel or short-term rental, a bed bug sighting is a reputational emergency — the kind of thing that ends up in a review before it ends up fixed. We prioritise hospitality enquiries for fast response.

Heat treatment vs. chemical treatment

  • Chemical treatment — targeted insecticide application to mattresses, frames, skirting and cracks. Usually needs 2 sessions roughly 2 weeks apart to catch eggs that hatch after the first visit.
  • Heat treatment — the room is heated to a temperature that kills bed bugs at every life stage in a single session. Faster and more thorough, but priced higher, and typically used for larger or more established infestations.

For hotels, a proactive inspection programme (not just reactive treatment after a guest complaint) is the standard approach among hospitality-focused operators.

FAQ

Common questions

How do I know it's bed bugs and not another insect?
Small reddish-brown bites in a line or cluster, tiny blood spots on sheets, and a musty odour near the mattress seams are the classic signs. A professional inspection is the reliable way to confirm before committing to treatment.
Do I need to throw out my mattress?
Usually not — professional treatment (chemical or heat) is designed to treat the mattress in place. Your matched operator will advise if disposal is genuinely necessary for a specific case.
How long does treatment take to fully work?
Chemical treatment typically needs a first visit plus a follow-up ~2 weeks later to catch newly hatched eggs. Heat treatment is designed to resolve an infestation in a single session, at a higher upfront cost.
