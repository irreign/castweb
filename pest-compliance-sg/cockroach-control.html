Cockroach control

The single most common pest complaint in Singapore homes and F&B premises — and the pest every SFA contract must cover at minimum. Here's what treatment actually involves and what it should cost.

Get matched with a partner

What treatment usually involves

  • Gel baiting along known harbourage points — behind appliances, under sinks, along skirting
  • Crack-and-crevice treatment in kitchens and drains
  • A follow-up visit within 2–4 weeks to confirm the population has dropped
  • For F&B premises: monthly recurring visits to satisfy SFA's minimum coverage requirement

DIY vs. calling a professional

Off-the-shelf gel bait and sprays can knock down a small, visible population. If roaches keep coming back within weeks, or you're seeing them during the day (a sign of a large, established population), that's the point where a professional visit pays for itself.

FAQ

Common questions

Are the chemicals used safe around food and kids?
NEA-certified operators use approved products and application methods designed for food-prep and residential environments. Ask your matched partner for the specific product data sheet if you need it for a health-sensitive household or kitchen.
How fast will roaches disappear after treatment?
Visible activity usually drops within days, but a full population knockdown (including eggs hatching later) typically takes 2–4 weeks and a follow-up visit.
Does my F&B outlet need a contract, or is one treatment enough?
For SFA compliance, no — a one-off treatment doesn't satisfy the requirement. You need a contract with at least monthly visits sustained over the term. See our SFA checklist tool.
