What a typical contract covers

Common areas, on a schedule

⌂

Common areas

Corridors, chutes, basements, drains and landscaping — the areas the MCST is responsible for, distinct from what's inside individual units.

📅

Bi-monthly + monthly

Most estates run bi-monthly general pest treatment for the common areas, plus a monthly mosquito-control round given Singapore's dengue-prevention requirements.

💵

Procured by the MA

The managing agent typically bundles pest control into the same procurement cycle as lifts, security and cleaning — renewed on a set schedule, not ad hoc.

Why this is worth re-tendering

The vendor you inherited may not be the vendor you'd pick

  • Watch for the classic "$50/month" plan that turns out to be a quarterly visit billed at S$150+ — always confirm visits-per-month and what each visit actually covers before signing.
  • NEA certification should be current and declared — ask for it in writing as part of any tender response.
  • Mosquito control isn't optional in Singapore's dengue-prevention context — confirm it's a distinct, monthly line item, not folded vaguely into "general pest control."
