# MentorMe Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [DATE — fill in before publishing]_

This policy explains what MentorMe collects, why, and what control you have over it. It's written in plain language on purpose — if anything here is unclear, that's a bug in the policy, not in your understanding.

## What we collect

**Account details:** your name, mobile number, and email address, entered once when you create your account.

**Life-stage details:** answers to short questionnaires shown the first time you visit the Home, Cards, and Grow tabs — things like your housing type, household status, career stage, income band, and (only if relevant) your children's ages. These are asked once per topic and remembered after.

**Usage data:** which recommendations you've accepted or dismissed, so we don't keep showing you things you've already acted on or told us aren't relevant.

We do **not** collect your bank account balances, passwords, or any financial account credentials. Where the app shows figures like "your balance may be near $200k," that's an estimate based on your self-reported income band, not data we've pulled from any bank.

## Why we collect it

- To place you on the "line" and show recommendations relevant to your actual situation, rather than generic content.
- To remember your answers so we don't ask twice.
- To send you a notification only when something you'd actually want to know about changes — a fee waiver deadline, an offer ending, a threshold you've crossed.

## Marketing consent

We only contact you for marketing purposes (e.g. "a new milestone applies to you") if you've explicitly opted in, via the toggle on your Profile screen. You can turn this off at any time, and doing so takes effect immediately. We keep a timestamped record of when you granted or withdrew this consent, so we can demonstrate compliance if ever asked — this record itself is not used for any other purpose.

## What we don't do

- We don't sell your data to anyone.
- We don't share your data with banks, credit card issuers, or investment platforms for their marketing purposes.
- We don't use your data to train third-party AI models.

## Where your data lives

Your data is stored with a managed database provider (Supabase) using industry-standard access controls, so that only you can read your own profile and account data. Push notifications, where enabled, are delivered through Expo's notification service, which only receives what's needed to route the notification to your device — not your profile data.

## Your rights

You can, at any time, from the Profile screen:
- **Download your data** — a copy of everything we hold about you.
- **Delete your account** — this removes your profile, contact details, and history. Some records (like the consent log) may be retained in de-identified form where required for legal or audit purposes.

If you're in Singapore, these rights are consistent with the Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA). If you're elsewhere, we aim to honour the same standard regardless of local requirement.

## Not financial or medical advice

MentorMe gives general information and comparisons — historical rates, public offer terms, common practices — not personalised financial advice, and not medical advice. Always confirm current terms with the relevant bank or provider, and consult a qualified professional for advice specific to your situation.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects how your data is used, we'll notify you in the app before the change takes effect, not just by quietly updating this page.
