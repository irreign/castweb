Common Questions

You've got questions.
We've got answers.

Do I need to provide content? +
Not necessarily. We work with whatever you have — phone photos, old brochures, or nothing at all. Basic copywriting and stock imagery are included in every package.
Can I update the site myself? +
Yes. We hand you full access and a simple guide. Or subscribe to our maintenance plan and we'll handle all updates for you.
Is hosting included in the price? +
The one-time fee covers the build. Hosting runs S$15–30/month, which we help you set up — we don't mark it up.
What if I don't like the design? +
Unlimited revision rounds. We don't go live until you've signed off and you're 100% happy.
Do you accept PayNow? +
Yes — PayNow, bank transfer, and credit card all accepted. For e-commerce clients we integrate PayNow, PayLah!, GrabPay, and Stripe into your store.
Will it work on mobile? +
Every site is mobile-first, tested on iPhone and Android. Over 78% of SG web traffic is mobile — non-negotiable for us.
